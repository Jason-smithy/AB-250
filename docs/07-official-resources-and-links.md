## 💡 Beta Exam Strategies & FAQs

### How are Beta Exams scored?
- Beta exams contain between 40–60 questions and include unscored experimental questions.
- Scores for beta exams are **not** provided immediately upon exam completion. Microsoft conducts statistical analysis and psychometric evaluation before publishing final scaled scores (typically when the exam goes live).
- Passing score is standard **700 / 1000**.

### Best Practices for Exam Day
1. **Hands-On Practice**: Set up a Microsoft 365 E5 trial with Dynamics 365 Contact Center and Copilot Studio to test routing rules, voice agents, and app profiles firsthand.
2. **Review the Skills Measured Matrix**: Prioritize high-weight domains (Implement Channels at 30–35% and Deploy Contact Center at 15–20%).
3. **Leave Detailed Item Feedback**: During beta exams, you can submit comments on question clarity and technical accuracy, directly shaping the final exam release.
